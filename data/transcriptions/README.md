## Editorial markers

- `[INCOMPLETE—continuation not photographed]` means the photographed page says
  that its ingredient list continues, but that continuation is absent.
- Editorial notes are not cookbook text and are labeled as such.
- Printed line wrapping is preserved where useful, but Markdown headings and
  source-image labels are editorial structure.
